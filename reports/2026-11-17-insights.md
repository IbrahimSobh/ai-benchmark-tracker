# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-17

Written from [2026-11-17-report.md](2026-11-17-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-17). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

No single model leads every table. Claude models lead four of the six test tables, while Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemini 4 Argon lead the other two. In several of these tables the runner-up is too close to call, and on the terminal test no margin is published. Separately, in public votes on the two arena tables, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both, though Claude Opus 5.5 is too close to call on one of them.

## Insights

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but three other models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **Claude Opus 5 leads SWE-Bench Pro V2, but three other models are too close to call.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 is a test of fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 scores 99.4%, and Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%) are too close to call. The setups differ between rows: Claude Opus 5 ran with Claude Code, xhigh, and Gemini 4 Argon with mini-swe-agent, high. The top 12 rows run from 99.4% to 95.5%, and the publisher data is dated 2026-11-06. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

3. **Kimi K3.1 Pro leads Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 67.2%, and no margin is published for any model behind it.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 is a test of coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Gemini 4 Carbon is next at 65.2%, and Claude Sonnet 5.5 is at 63.6%. Because no margin is published, the gap to the leader is not established. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

4. **Separately, in public votes on Agent Arena and WebDev Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both, but on WebDev Arena Claude Opus 5.5 is too close to call.** Agent Arena is public votes where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, and WebDev Arena is public votes where people compare websites and apps built by two models. On Agent Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 scores 0.286 at a median of $9.02 per task, against 0.182 at $5.65 for Claude Opus 5.5. Its Agent Arena score changed by -0.017 since the earlier update. On WebDev Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 scores 1826 and Claude Opus 5.5 scores 1816. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent)) ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev))
