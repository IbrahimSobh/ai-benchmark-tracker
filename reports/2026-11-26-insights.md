# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-26

Written from [2026-11-26-report.md](2026-11-26-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-26). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

Claude models lead five of the seven tables with data this update, but in four of those five the table marks other models as too close to call. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads Terminal-Bench 4.0, and Gemini 4 Argon leads MCP-Atlas. GDPx by SemiAnalysis has no data this update because its collector failed. Public votes on Agent Arena put Claude Fable 5.2 first, at the highest median cost per task among the twelve models shown.

## Insights

1. **Claude models lead SWE Atlas, but five other models are too close to call with the leader.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%), Gemini 4 Neon (72.0%), Gemini 4 Hydrogen (72.0%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. Claude models hold the first three places. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **SWE-Bench Pro V2 no longer separates the top models.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 measures fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 leads with 99.4%, and three other models are too close to call with it: Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%). Twelve models are shown, from 99.4% down to 95.5%. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

3. **Terminal-Bench 4.0 has a leader, but the table publishes no margin.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 tests coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads with 67.2%, with Gemini 4 Carbon at 65.2% and GPT-6.2 Astra at 64.1% next. No margin is published for any model below the leader, so this table cannot show whether the lead is clear. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

4. **Public votes on Agent Arena favor Claude Fable 5.2, at the highest median cost in the top twelve.** Agent Arena is a public vote: people rate AI agents on their own real tasks. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with a score of 0.274 at a median cost of $9.15 per task. Gemini 4 Carbon scores 0.161 at $1.35 per task, and Claude Opus 5.5 Max scores 0.191 at $5.79. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent))
