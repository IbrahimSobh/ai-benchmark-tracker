# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-28

Written from [2026-11-28-report.md](2026-11-28-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-28). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

Claude models lead the SWE Atlas, SWE-Bench Pro V2 and GDPval-AA tables, but several rivals are too close to call in the coding tests. The top of SWE-Bench Pro V2 is nearly tied, so that test separates few leaders. Terminal-Bench 4.0 is led by Kimi K3.1 Pro, and no margin is published. Separately, in public votes on Agent Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 leads and has the highest median cost per task of its top four models. GDPx by SemiAnalysis has no data in this update.

## Insights

1. **On SWE Atlas, Claude Fable 5.2 leads, but five models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%), Gemini 4 Neon (72.0%), Gemini 4 Hydrogen (72.0%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Kimi K3.1 Pro leads at 67.2%, and no margin is published.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 measures coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Gemini 4 Carbon follows at 65.2%, and Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Gemini 4 Neon both score 63.6%. No margin is published for any model in this table, so the table does not show whether the leader's lead is clear. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

3. **SWE-Bench Pro V2 is nearly saturated at the top.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 measures fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 leads with 99.4%, but three other models are too close to call: Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%). All twelve models shown score 95.5% or higher, so this test barely separates the leaders. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

4. **In public votes, Claude Fable 5.2 leads Agent Arena, and it costs the most per task among the top four.** Agent Arena is a public-vote leaderboard where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with a score of 0.274 at a median cost of $9.15 per task. Gemini 4 Neon scores 0.183 at $2.48 per task, and Gemini 4 Carbon scores 0.161 at $1.35 per task. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent))
