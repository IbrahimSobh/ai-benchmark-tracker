# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-13

Written from [2026-11-13-report.md](2026-11-13-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-13). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

This is the first update, so there are no changes to compare yet. Across the six test tables, the leader changes by task: Claude Fable 5.2 leads two, Claude Opus 5 and Claude Opus 5.5 each lead one, and Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemini 4 Argon each lead one. Several leads are too close to call, and two of the test tables publish no margin. Separately, in public votes on two tables, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both.

## Insights

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but three other models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 4 Carbon are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **Claude Opus 5 leads SWE-Bench Pro V2, and the top of the table is crowded.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 tests fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 leads with 99.4%, but Gemini 4 Argon, Claude Fable 5.1 and Inkling 2 Small are too close to call. The 12 rows shown run from 99.4% down to 95.5%, so the top of this table separates models less clearly. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

3. **Claude Fable 5.2 is first on SWE Atlas and on GDPx.** GDPx is a set of very hard expert tasks in fields such as chip design, finance and law. Data from GDPx by SemiAnalysis shows Claude Fable 5.2 at 16.4% and $88.15 per task, the highest cost of the three models shown. No margin is published for the models behind it there, so the gap is not established. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas)) ([source](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/))

4. **Claude Fable 5.2 also leads both public-vote tables.** Agent Arena is public votes where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, and WebDev Arena is public votes where people compare websites and apps built by two models. On Agent Arena it scores 0.303, and no other model is too close to call. On WebDev Arena it scores 1838, but Claude Opus 5.5 (1815) is too close to call. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent)) ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev))
