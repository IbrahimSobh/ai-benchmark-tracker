# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-13

Written from [2026-11-13-report.md](2026-11-13-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-13). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

This is the first update, so there is nothing to compare yet. No single model leads every table, and the leaders change from one test to the next. Claude models lead most of the tests, but Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemini 4 Argon lead the other two. Several first places are too close to call or have no margin published, so one clear winner does not appear in every table. Separately, in public votes, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both vote tables (too close to call on WebDev Arena).

## Insights

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but three models are too close to call with it.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 is first at 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.4%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.4%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.4%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **The top of SWE-Bench Pro V2 is crowded.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 is a test of fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 leads with 99.4%, but Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%) are too close to call with it. All 12 scores shown fall between 95.5% and 99.4%. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

3. **Terminal-Bench 4.0 has a leader, but no margin is published below it.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 is coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads with 67.2%, and Gemini 4 Carbon is second with 65.2%. No margin is published, so the gap between them is not called clear. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

4. **On GDPx, the leader also costs the most per task.** GDPx is very hard expert tasks in fields such as chip design, finance and law. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 16.4% at $88.15 per task. Claude Opus 5.5 (12.0%, $51.41) and Gemini 4 Carbon (10.9%, $16.29) score lower, and no margin is published for either. ([source](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/), GDPx by SemiAnalysis)
