# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-14

Written from [2026-11-14-report.md](2026-11-14-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-14). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

This is the second update, and only one test table has changes since 2026-11-13, and they are small. No single model leads every test table: Claude Fable 5.2 leads two of the six, and four other models lead the rest. Several leads are too close to call, and two test tables publish no margin for the models behind the leader. Separately, in public votes on the two arena tables, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both.

## Insights

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but three other models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.4%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.4%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.4%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **Kimi K3.1 Pro leads Terminal-Bench 4.0, but no margin is published, so its gap is not established.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 is a test of coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro scores 67.2%, and for Gemini 4 Carbon (65.2%) and every model below it the table says no margin is published. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

3. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads GDPx, and it is the most expensive of the three models shown.** GDPx is a set of very hard expert tasks in fields such as chip design, finance and law. Claude Fable 5.2 scores 16.4% at $88.15 per task, while Gemini 4 Carbon scores 10.9% at $16.29 per task, and no margin is published for either model behind the leader. GDPx by SemiAnalysis ([source](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/))

4. **Separately, in public votes on Agent Arena and WebDev Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both.** Agent Arena is public votes where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, and WebDev Arena is public votes where people compare websites and apps built by two models. On Agent Arena it scores 0.303 at a median of $7.43 per task, against 0.170 at $1.46 for Gemini 4 Carbon, and no other model is too close to call. On WebDev Arena it scores 1838, but Claude Opus 5.5 (1815) is too close to call. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent)) ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev))
