## Method

- **Trust labels.** *independent*: a third party ran the models. *crowd-preference*: ranking from public human votes. Vendor-reported scores are never mixed in.
- **Too close to call.** Shown when a model's published 95% interval overlaps the leader's. Where a publisher shows no interval, the table says *no margin published*.
- **GDPx score** is GDPx's own formula (equal weight per domain and per partner) applied to the data GDPx publishes; it matches the numbers on gdpx.semianalysis.com. Credit: GDPx by SemiAnalysis.
- **Arena** data comes from Arena's official dataset ([lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset), CC-BY-4.0).
