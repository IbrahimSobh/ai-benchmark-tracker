# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-19

Written from [2026-11-19-report.md](2026-11-19-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-19). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

Claude models lead four of the six test tables, but no model leads them all. Gemini 4 Argon and Kimi K3.1 Pro lead the other two. In all six test tables, the runner-up is either too close to call or has no published margin. Separately, in public votes on two Arena leaderboards, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both.

## Insights

1. **Claude leads both coding tests, but the top of the SWE-Bench Pro table is crowded.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads it with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas)) SWE-Bench Pro V2 is a test of fixing real issues in software projects. Its top 12 rows run from 99.4% down to 95.5%. Claude Opus 5 leads with 99.4%, but Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%) are too close to call. The setups differ between rows, so compare them with care. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

2. **Gemini 4 Argon and Kimi K3.1 Pro lead the two tests where no Claude model is first.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 is a test of coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads with 67.2%, and no margin is published for this table; the highest Claude row is Claude Sonnet 5.5 at 63.6%. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0)) MCP-Atlas is a test of using tools and apps through MCP connections to finish multi-step tasks. Gemini 4 Argon leads with 90.7%, but Muse Spark 1.1 (88.1%) and Claude Fable 5.1 (87.2%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/mcp_atlas))

3. **Claude leads both work tests, and on GDPx the top score also has the highest cost per task.** GDPval-AA is a test of realistic office-work tasks, rated with a chess-style Elo score. Claude Opus 5.5 leads with 1866, but Kimi K3.1 Pro (1861), Muse Wonder Full (1857), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (1838) and Claude Fable 5.2 (1831) are too close to call. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa)) GDPx is a test of very hard expert tasks in fields such as chip design, finance and law. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 16.4% at $88.15 per task, against 11.9% at $51.87 for Claude Opus 5.5 and 10.9% at $16.29 for Gemini 4 Carbon; no margin is published for either runner-up. In these three rows, the highest score also has the highest cost per task. ([source](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/), GDPx by SemiAnalysis)

4. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads both public-vote tables, but Claude Opus 5.5 is too close to call on WebDev Arena.** Agent Arena is public votes where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, and WebDev Arena is public votes where people compare websites and apps built by two models. On Agent Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 scores 0.274 at a median of $9.16 per task, against 0.161 at $1.33 for Gemini 4 Carbon, and its change since the earlier update is -0.029. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent)) On WebDev Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 scores 1824 and Claude Opus 5.5 scores 1818, which is too close to call. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev))
