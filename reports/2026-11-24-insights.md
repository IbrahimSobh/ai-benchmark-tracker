# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-24

Written from [2026-11-24-report.md](2026-11-24-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-24). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

The leader changes from test to test, and at the top of several tables the gaps are not clear. Claude models lead SWE Atlas and SWE-Bench Pro V2, while Kimi K3.1 Pro leads Terminal-Bench 4.0. Separately, in public votes on Agent Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 leads, but that ranking measures people's preferences, not test scores.

## Insights

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but five models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%), Gemini 4 Neon (72.0%), Gemini 4 Hydrogen (72.0%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **SWE-Bench Pro V2 is bunched at the top, with Claude Opus 5 in front.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 is about fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 leads with 99.4%, but Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

3. **Terminal-Bench 4.0 has a leader, but no margin is published behind it.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 measures coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads with 67.2%. Gemini 4 Carbon is next at 65.2%, but no margin is published, so the order below the leader is not confirmed. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

4. **On Agent Arena, the top score also costs the most per task among the top 12.** Agent Arena is a public-vote ranking where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, so this is a vote result, not a test score. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 0.274 at a median $9.15 per task. Gemini 4 Neon scores 0.183 at $2.48, and Gemini 4 Carbon scores 0.161 at $1.35. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent))
