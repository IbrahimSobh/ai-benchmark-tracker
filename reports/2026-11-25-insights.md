# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-25

Written from [2026-11-25-report.md](2026-11-25-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-25). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

Each table has a leader, but in several of them the models behind it are too close to call. Claude models lead the coding and office-work tests, and Kimi K3.1 Pro leads the terminal test, where no margin is published. Separately, in public votes on Agent Arena (people rate AI agents on their own real tasks), Claude Fable 5.2 leads, but that ranking measures people's preferences, not test scores.

## Insights

1. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but five other models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%), Gemini 4 Neon (72.0%), Gemini 4 Hydrogen (72.0%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

2. **On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Kimi K3.1 Pro leads, but no margin is published.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 measures coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads with 67.2%, and Gemini 4 Carbon is next at 65.2%. Because no margin is published, the gap between them is not confirmed, and neither is the order below the leader. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0))

3. **On GDPval-AA, Claude Opus 5.5 leads with 1866, but four other models are too close to call.** GDPval-AA covers realistic office-work tasks, rated with a chess-style Elo score. Muse Wonder Full (1857), Kimi K3.1 Pro (1856), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (1838) and Claude Fable 5.2 (1830) are too close to call with Claude Opus 5.5. The Change column shows -4.8 for Kimi K3.1 Pro. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa))

4. **In public votes, Claude Fable 5.2 leads Agent Arena and is also the costliest per task among the top 12.** Agent Arena is a public-vote ranking where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, so this is a vote result, not a test score. Claude Fable 5.2 scores 0.274 at a median $9.15 per task. Claude Opus 5.5 (Max) scores 0.191 at a median $5.79 per task. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent))
