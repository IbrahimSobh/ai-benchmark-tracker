# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-23

Written from [2026-11-23-report.md](2026-11-23-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-23). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

Claude models lead four of the six test tables, while Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemini 4 Argon lead the other two. Many runners-up are too close to call, and several tables publish no margin at all. In GDPx, the top score also has the highest cost per task of the three rows shown. Separately, in public votes on two Arena leaderboards, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both.

## Insights

1. **Claude leads both coding tests, but the top of each table is crowded.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads it with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.5%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.5%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.5%) are too close to call. Scale says v1.1 scores are not comparable with v1.0. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas)) SWE-Bench Pro V2 is a test of fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 leads it with 99.4%, but Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

2. **Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemini 4 Argon lead the two tests where Claude is not first.** Terminal-Bench 4.0 is a test of coding tasks done in a computer terminal. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads it with 67.2%, and the table publishes no margin for the rows behind it. The highest Claude row in the top 12 shown is Claude Sonnet 5.5 at 63.6%. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/terminalbench-4-0)) MCP-Atlas is a test of using tools and apps through MCP connections to finish multi-step tasks. Gemini 4 Argon leads it with 90.7%, but Muse Spark 1.1 (88.1%) and Claude Fable 5.1 (87.2%) are too close to call. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/mcp_atlas))

3. **Claude leads both work tests, and on GDPx the top score also has the highest cost per task.** GDPval-AA is a test of realistic office-work tasks, rated with a chess-style Elo score. Claude Opus 5.5 leads it with 1866, but Kimi K3.1 Pro (1861), Muse Wonder Full (1857), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (1838) and Claude Fable 5.2 (1831) are too close to call. ([source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa)) GDPx is a test of very hard expert tasks in fields such as chip design, finance and law. GDPx by SemiAnalysis shows Claude Fable 5.2 leading with 16.4% at $88.15 per task, against 11.9% at $51.87 for Claude Opus 5.5 and 10.9% at $16.29 for Gemini 4 Carbon. No margin is published for the two runners-up. ([source](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/), GDPx by SemiAnalysis)

4. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads both public-vote tables.** Agent Arena is a public-vote table where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks. Claude Fable 5.2 scores 0.274 at a median cost of $9.15 per task. Claude Opus 5.5 is second at 0.191, and Gemini 4 Neon is third at 0.183. Its Change value is -0.012. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent)) WebDev Arena is a public-vote table where people compare websites and apps built by two models. Claude Fable 5.2 leads it with 1822, and Claude Opus 5.5 (1820) is too close to call. Its Change value is -3.9. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev))
