# Frontier AI benchmarks: summary and insights, update of 2026-11-16

Written from [2026-11-16-report.md](2026-11-16-report.md) (data collected 2026-11-16). If that tables file is collected again, check these insights still match.

## Summary

No single model leads every test table. Claude Opus 5 leads the issue-fixing test, but three other models are too close to call, and Claude Fable 5.2 leads the professional coding test with three other models too close to call. Kimi K3.1 Pro leads the terminal test, and no margin is published for any model behind it. Separately, in public votes on the two arena tables, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both, though Claude Opus 5.5 is too close to call on the web development one.

## Insights

1. **Claude Opus 5 leads SWE-Bench Pro V2, but three other models are too close to call.** SWE-Bench Pro V2 is a test of fixing real issues in software projects. Claude Opus 5 scores 99.4%, and Gemini 4 Argon (99.2%), Claude Fable 5.1 (99.1%) and Inkling 2 Small (97.7%) are too close to call. The top 12 rows shown run from 99.4% to 95.5%, so this test is close to its ceiling and separates the leaders poorly. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2))

2. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads SWE Atlas, but three other models are too close to call.** SWE Atlas is Scale's test of professional coding work: answering questions about a codebase, writing tests and refactoring. Claude Fable 5.2 leads with 80.5%, but Claude Opus 5.5 (77.4%), Claude Fable 5.1 (76.4%) and Gemini 4 Carbon (71.4%) are too close to call. Scale notes that v1.1 scores are not comparable with v1.0. ([source](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe-atlas))

3. **Claude Fable 5.2 leads GDPx, and it is the most expensive of the three models shown.** GDPx is a set of very hard expert tasks in fields such as chip design, finance and law. GDPx by SemiAnalysis ([source](https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/)) shows Claude Fable 5.2 at 16.4% for $88.15 per task, Claude Opus 5.5 at 12.0% for $51.41, and Gemini 4 Carbon at 10.9% for $16.29. No margin is published for either model behind the leader, so the gap to them is not established.

4. **Separately, in public votes on Agent Arena and WebDev Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 leads both.** Agent Arena is public votes where people rate AI agents on their own real tasks, and WebDev Arena is public votes where people compare websites and apps built by two models. On Agent Arena, Claude Fable 5.2 scores 0.286 at a median of $9.02 per task, against 0.182 at $5.65 for Claude Opus 5.5. On WebDev Arena it scores 1826, but Claude Opus 5.5 (1816) is too close to call. ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/agent)) ([source](https://arena.ai/leaderboard/code/webdev))
